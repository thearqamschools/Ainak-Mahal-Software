# Ainak Mahal optical store software

**A clearer view of your shop.**

From the first frame to the final fitting, keep customers, prescriptions, stock and optical
jobs together in one Windows desktop application. Find the right item, record an advance,
follow a pair through the lab and collect the balance when it is ready.

Built for Ainak Mahal at Millat Chowk, Faisalabad. Works offline, keeps store data on your
computer and needs no hosting subscription. Print invoices, review daily activity and keep
verified backups on a drive you control.
